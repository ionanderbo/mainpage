CardioPatch: 24/7 Wearable Cardiac Patch for Silent Heart Attack Warning

Co-designed in a 4-person biomedical engineering team (Group 06, University of Deusto) to solve a clear clinical gap: nearly 45% of myocardial infarctions occur without classic chest pain, while retrospective Holter patches only report data days later.

We architected a reusable IP67 shower-proof electronics hub paired with disposable hypoallergenic silicone patches. Guided by stakeholder interviews with cardiology and cardiac rehab nursing, the system runs lightweight on-device AI to suppress false alarms during stair climbing while escalating real ST-segment shifts and skin-temperature drops with a 30-second ECG strip.

Lung Nodule Characterisation & CT-Guided Biopsy Planning

Segmented a solid pulmonary nodule on chest CT in 3D Slicer, reconstructed a 3D anatomical model, planned a single-pleura-crossing needle path avoiding ribs and major vessels, recovered a 10 mm simulated displacement via mutual-information rigid registration, and extracted radiomic features.

Critical limits learned: A raw mean intensity of −171.8 HU looked artificially low for soft tissue because 17.5% of segmented voxels captured aerated lung along the boundary rim. At ~18 mm equivalent diameter, the lesion exceeds the 8 mm Fleischner threshold, justifying biopsy planning while highlighting the need for respiratory motion compensation.

EEG Signal Quality & Artifact Rejection

Audited sampling and aliasing, ranked 14 scalp channels by electrode contact quality, applied a 1–40 Hz bandpass filter, and isolated ocular eye-blink artifacts using synchronous accelerometer data. Removing a single noisy electrode (P7) rescued recording usability from 26.60% to over 70%.
