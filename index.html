Lung nodule characterisation and CT-guided biopsy planning

With three teammates I segmented a solid nodule on a chest CT, built a 3D model, planned a virtual needle path, registered a displaced scan back to the original and extracted radiomic features. The path crosses the pleura once and avoids ribs and vessels.

The most useful lesson was about the numbers. A mean intensity of −171.8 HU looks far too low for soft tissue, but 17.5% of the segment’s voxels were aerated lung from its rim, so the value described my segmentation more than the lesion. At about 18 mm the nodule is above the 8 mm Fleischner threshold for further work-up, which supports planning a biopsy.

EEG signal quality and artifact rejection

I ranked 14 channels by contact quality, band-pass filtered between 1 and 40 Hz and classified eye-blink artifacts using the accelerometer. Dropping the worst channel, P7, is what rescues the recording.

All 14 channels: 26.60% usable
Without P7: over 70% usable
